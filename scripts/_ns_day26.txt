Q: Given a number N = 12345678901234567890... (up to 50 digits). What is the smallest number that should be added to N so that the sum is exactly divisible by 11?
Opts: ['6', '3', '5', '8']
CA: 3
Sol: # Solution:

**Step 1:** Identify the pattern of the 50-digit number.
- N = 123456789012345678901234567890... (repeating pattern of 1-9-0)
- The pattern "1234567890" repeats 5 times to make 50 digits

**Step 2:** Apply the divisibility rule for 11.
- A number is divisible by 11 if the difference between the sum of digits at odd positions and even positions is divisible by 11.
- Alternating sum = (sum of odd position digits) - (sum of even position digits)

**Step 3:** Calculate the alternating sum for one complete cycle "1234567890".
- Odd positions (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th): 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 25
- Even positions (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th): 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 0 = 20
- Alternating sum per cycle = 25 - 20 = 5

**Step 4:** Calculate total alternating sum for 5 complete cycles.
- Total alternating sum = 5 × 5 = 25

**Step 5:** Find the remainder when divided by 11.
- 25 = 11 × 2 + 3
- Remainder = 3
